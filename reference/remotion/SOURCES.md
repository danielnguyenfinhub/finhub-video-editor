# remotion-sources

Text-only reference copy of selected parts of https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion
Upstream commit: 152fb7e (shallow clone, copied 2026-10-07). Binary media (images, video, audio, fonts, wasm) removed.

Contents: packages/docs/docs and packages/docs/elements (documentation and Elements), packages/skills (agent skills),
effects, shapes, transitions, light-leaks, starburst, sfx, captions, fonts, design, rounded-text-box, rough-notation,
animation-utils, motion-blur, noise, paths, layout-utils, and every template-* package.

Location in finhub-video-editor: reference/remotion/ (excluded from tsc; never imported by src/). Harness skill source path.

Purpose: source material for designing FinHub video templates (finhub-video-editor) through the harness skill.
Not a build: nothing here is installed or run. Use the published @remotion/* packages at the version pinned in finhub-video-editor.

Licence: Remotion's own licence applies (LICENSE.md). Free for individuals and small companies; larger for-profit
organisations need a company licence. FinHub's position is unverified: check before using any of this in a product.
