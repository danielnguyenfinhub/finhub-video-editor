GLOBAL RE LISTINGS — THÔNG TIN NHÀ ĐỂ LÀM VIDEO / LISTINGS FOR VIDEOS

Three steps / Ba bước:

1. Copy the "_TEMPLATE" folder and rename the copy after the property,
   for example "12 Smith Street Cabramatta".
   Chép thư mục "_TEMPLATE" rồi đổi tên theo căn nhà.

2. In that folder: fill in listing.txt (open it with Notepad, write after each
   colon) and put the property's photos next to it (jpg or png).
   Trong thư mục đó: điền listing.txt (mở bằng Notepad, viết sau dấu hai chấm)
   và bỏ ảnh căn nhà vào cùng chỗ (jpg hoặc png).
   iPhone HEIC photos are skipped: set Settings > Camera > Formats > Most Compatible.

3. In the Claude Code chat, say: "Make the Global RE video for 12 Smith Street".
   Trong khung chat Claude Code, nói: "Làm video Global RE cho 12 Smith Street".

Claude checks the details, looks at every photo, writes the script (you approve
it before it is voiced), and puts the finished Vietnamese and English videos
and the caption in "4 - GLOBAL RE FINISHED VIDEOS".

Only true facts: the video says only what listing.txt says or what the photos
show. The price is written exactly as advertised; never "Offers over".

Nothing in this folder goes to GitHub (only this note and the template do).
