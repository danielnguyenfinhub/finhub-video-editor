YOUR FINISHED VIDEOS APPEAR HERE

After Claude renders a video, two files land here, named after its topic:

  <Topic>.mp4               the finished video (9:16: Reels, TikTok, Facebook)
  <Topic> - caption.txt     the caption to paste when you post: title, text,
                            your contact details, 7 hashtags (#finhub
                            #vietnamese and 5 about the topic), then the
                            licence and disclaimer footer that must stay on
                            every post

Watch the video and read the caption before you post. Ask Claude in the chat
for any change; a re-render replaces both files.

If a video does not appear here, the render message says what to fix in its
caption (usually the hashtags) and the command that puts it here.

The other sizes (Facebook feed 4:5, a smaller phone copy, the thumbnail and
subtitles) stay in out/videos/<slug>/ if you need them.

Nothing in this folder goes to GitHub (only this note does).
