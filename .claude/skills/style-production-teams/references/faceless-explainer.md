# Recipe: faceless, explainer and process styles

Styles: blueprint, faceless, isometric, journey, kinetic, orbit, paper, phoneapp, retro, whiteboard. Pipeline B (voiced script, no cut-out). `kinetic` is low render cost, the other nine med.

**Pick this team when** the video explains how something works: intents `explain`, `process`, `hook`, `myth`; shapes `steps`, `narrative`, `comparison`. `kinetic` also suits a hook-led myth-buster.

**Differences that decide the pick:** `phoneapp` has a 50-character card limit (the others 60); `retro` is the only one with `promote` and `engage` intents; `kinetic` is the only low-cost one. The look of the rest (steps, drawn, isometric, orbital, blueprint) is inferred from their names and unverified: read each `template.json` `grammar` and its preview before promising a look.

**Process:** same as the faceless data team (script → compliance → Daniel locks → voice with the cost gate → build → QC ‖ compliance). Copy to `maxChars` and `minHoldMs` in both languages before voicing; real Vietnamese diacritics, NFC.

**Rules that bite:** no promise language ("tốt nhất", "miễn phí", "đảm bảo", "best", "free", "guaranteed"); eligibility explained as general information, never as personal advice; safe band for every element.
